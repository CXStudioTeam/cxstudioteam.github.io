How it works

The classic pomodoro rhythm, automated. Three steps, repeated.

1

Start a work session

Pick a task and focus for 25 minutes — or any length you choose. Ticking sounds and white noise keep you in the zone.

2

Reward yourself with a break

When the session ends, take a short break. Customize break lengths to fit your rhythm.

3

Take a long break, then repeat

After a few rounds, enjoy a longer break. Review your charts and watch the focused hours add up.

Questions? Look here.

Everything else you might want to know before your first session.

What is the pomodoro technique?

You work in focused intervals — traditionally 25 minutes — separated by short breaks. Brain Focus automates the timing, reminders and breaks so you can just start and focus.

Does Brain Focus work offline?

Yes. The timer, tasks and reports work completely offline. An internet connection is only needed for optional features like Google Drive backup.

How does App Lock help me focus?

While a work session is running, distracting apps you choose show a "Stay focused!" screen instead of their content — a gentle nudge back to work every time you slip.

How do I back up my data?

Back up your tasks and settings locally or to Google Drive, and restore them on any device. Your focus history travels with you.

What do I get with Pro?

Pro removes ads and unlocks dark mode, all colorful themes, the retro flip clock, every white noise sound and unlimited App Lock. It's a one-time upgrade inside the app.
